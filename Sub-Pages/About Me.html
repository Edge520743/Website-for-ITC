Hi, I am Emmanuel III D. Jayme

Hello, my name is Emmanuel III D. Jayme, I am 18 years old and a graduate from University of San Carlos - Senior High School. My prior school was actually Academia De San Jose (formerly named Saint Jospeh’s Academy).

My hobbies are programming, digital artistry, hand-drawn doodling, and short story writing. I am mostly a creative thinker type person and not really the critical thinker guy, but that setback shall not stop me from pushing forward.

Oh, if you're curious, the reason I took BSCS is because I always had interest in computers in general. I like how we can use them in our daily lives, tinker with them to our heart’s content, or notice how some computers can just fit in the palm of our hand. While I have some soft knowledge about how a computer’s hardware works in general, I know barely anything about the software side. Therefore, my reason joining this program is to learn more about how computers manage things in the software side, and also fulfill my dreams in creating my very first own software / game / website.

So what have I... done?

Besides completing junior high school education, and senior high school education, I have yet to accomplish grander things. For now, what I have accomplished is setting up myself to allow for free-lance art commissions, and preparing for what is to come in the future.

The only works I have done is artworks, which I have posted in all of my social media. I have also done stickers artworks for certain people who were interested, or I was interested. One day, I hope, I may be able to publish more works such as literary, and applications made from my experience in this course.
