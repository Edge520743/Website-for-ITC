An Intro to Computer Science

Computer science started way before the 1930s, one can even claim that it started even way back in 1800 BCE. In around the 1930 - 1960s, Computer Science was properly established as a discipline as there was a junction between Newton’s law of motion and Maxwell’s law of electromagnetism. The combination between algorithm theory, mathematical logic, and the stored-program electronic computer created the foundations for computer science as a discipline. In general, computer scientists create algorithms by answering theoretical problems. Algorithms in general are created to control and manage devices via their set instructions.

Areas of Computer Science

Computer science has six interrelated areas, which are:

  • Theory of Computation (such as analysis of algorithms)
  • Software (such as programming and compilers)
  • Hardware (such as logic design)
  • Resource Management (such as operating systems)
  • Computational Mathematics (such as numerical analysis)
  • Artificial Intelligence (such as natural language understanding)

An image of "Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz", known to be the founder of Computer Science as he formulated a model that computers still use to this day.

Issues with Computer Science

Despite Computer Science gaining a lot of traction, especially since it was quite a novel and new discipline back then, people had a lot of reservations about this so-called “discipline”:

  • People thought that computer science would not make it through 20 years since the whole discipline is focused on a machine which they thought would easily become obsolete.
  • Society labeled women under computer science to be more masculine than what was expected, and men under computer science to be less masculine than what was expected.

Information Systems

Mostly arose within schools of business. Defined as an integrated and cooperating set of software in order to support a certain goal.

Information Technology

It is the discipline of how information should be used and processed. Information Systems utilizes Information Technology in order to store and distribute information.

Computer Science

This discipline was established based on mathematics or engineering problems. Defined as the study of information processes. A “process” is a sequence of steps, one way to study these information processes is by using an algorithm, and an algorithm is described as the description of the process.

Computer Engineering

Defined as something related to a multivariable mathematical problem, wherein the engineer finds the most optimal solution to a problem. This discipline was actually part of electronics engineering, but was later made into a computing discipline.

Software Engineering

Defined as an application of a systematic, disciplined, quantifiable approach to the development, operation, and maintenance of software. This discipline was established for more precise methods of making and maintaining software.

Reflection

Based on what I have researched, I have gained new information and further improved my understanding from the topics I already knew some information about. For the topic “Computer Science as a Discipline”, I was quite intrigued that back then, people did not seem to like the discipline because it required the use of a “computer”, which people back then thought would become obsolete. Society also condemned anyone under computer science, having a belief that this discipline caused people to become “less masculine or feminine” and such. Obviously, society cannot stop the advancement of science, and computer science has stayed as a discipline until to this day. Computer science is a really interesting discipline since its structure and frameworks were built upon already existing mathematical theories and laws, making it really a one of its kind discipline in science. The culmination of already pre-existing disciplines helped cofounded computer science as one new unique discipline, which really shows how influential both sciences and mathematics are. Not only that, computer science also co-mingles with other computing disciplines, creating an interconnected web of sorts. On the other hand, for the topic “5 Computing Disciplines”, I have discovered that all of them all tie back together with each other. Computer science ties together with computer engineering, computer engineering ties with software engineering, those three disciplines tie with information systems, and lastly, information systems utilizes information technology. These disciplines co-mingle with each other since some areas they discuss overlap each other. They are the sustainers of our digital world today, creating new and novel ideas while maintaining them for years to come. Back then, people thought computers were just another fashion item, a technological marvel which would be buried in the footnotes of the history books as people would move on to the next hot thing. However, the disciplines remained not still, but steadfast in progressing computing. The combination of their efforts finally bore their fruit when the “computer” finally made it to everyone’s house and continued to remain there until to this day. Thus, society was proven wrong and it really was all because of these 5 co-disciplines working with each other. And to be honest, I really am astonished we made it this far still utilizing the key discoveries which lead to the very first creation of the computer system.
